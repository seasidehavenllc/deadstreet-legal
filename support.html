Dead Street Support

Dead Street is a side-scrolling zombie shooter from Seaside Haven LLC, on iPhone, iPad, Android and Steam.

Contact us: info@seasidehavenllc.com
Tell us your device (for example iPhone 15), the game version from Settings, and what happened. We usually reply within 2 days.

Common questions

Where is my progress saved?

On your device. If you sign in, it is also backed up to your account. Without signing in, deleting the app or clearing its data erases your progress.

How do I move my progress to a new phone?

Sign in on your old device, then sign in with the same account on the new one. Your progress comes with you.

An ad didn't give me my reward.

Rewards are only given when the ad plays to the end. If no ad loads, try again later or check your internet connection. If you watched the whole ad and got nothing, email us with the time it happened.

Can I turn off the blood?

Yes. Open Settings in the game and turn Blood off.

Can I use a game controller?

Yes. Pair a Bluetooth controller with your device and press any button in the game.

How do I delete my account or leaderboard name?

Email us from the address you signed in with, or with your leaderboard name, and we'll delete it.

How do I reset my game?

Open Settings and tap Reset Progress. This can't be undone.
