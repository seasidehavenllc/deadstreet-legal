Dead Street Privacy Policy

Dead Street is published by Seaside Haven LLC ("we", "us").

Last updated November 13, 2026

The short version

You can play Dead Street without an account. Your progress is saved on your own device. Signing in is optional and backs up your progress. A few features send data to the services described below. We never sell your data.

What the game stores on your device

Your cash, weapons, upgrades, level, settings and redeemed codes are saved in the game's storage on your device.

Optional account and cloud save

If you choose to sign in with Google, Apple or an email link, we receive your email address and a user ID from that sign-in service. We use them only to back up your progress and restore it on another device. Your account and saved progress are stored with Supabase, our database provider.

Worldwide leaderboard

When a run ends, the game sends your leaderboard name, the level and area you reached, your kill and cash records, your character and a random player ID to the Dead Street leaderboard, also stored with Supabase. This is shown publicly, so use a nickname, not your real name.

Ads (Android and iOS versions)

The mobile versions show ads from Google AdMob. Google may use your device's advertising ID (on iOS, only if you allow tracking when asked) and approximate location from your IP address to show and measure ads, as described in Google's advertising policy. Where the law requires it, the game asks for your consent first. Rewarded ads are always your choice. The Steam version shows no ads.

What the game does not do

No analytics of its own, no tracking cookies, and your data is never sold.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal information. It contains cartoon violence and blood, which can be turned off in Settings.

Deleting your data

Reset Progress in Settings erases your progress on your device. To delete your account, cloud save or leaderboard entry, email us from the address you signed in with, or with your leaderboard name, and we will delete it within 30 days.
